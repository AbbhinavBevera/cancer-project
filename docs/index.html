Methodology & Design Principles

1. Leak-Free Validation

High-dimensional transcriptomics data (p >> n) readily overfits. Feature filtering is locked strictly within cross-validation training folds, preventing premature test exposure.

2. Stability Selection

Rather than relying on single-fit regression coefficients, candidate genes are scored by recurrence across 50 resamples. Genes chosen in ≥95% of folds represent reproducible biomarkers.

3. Prioritizing Sensitivity

In oncology, failing to detect an active malignancy is far more hazardous than reviewing a false alert. Models are calibrated to ensure 100% recall on held-out test splits.
